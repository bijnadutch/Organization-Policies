## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out simple, everyday practices for keeping physical documents, removable media, and unattended computer screens free from casual or opportunistic exposure — whether in a XXAXX office, a remote working location, or a public space. Many data exposures happen not through sophisticated attacks but through a document left on a desk, a screen left unlocked, or a whiteboard photographed in the background of a video call.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff, wherever they are working — XXAXX offices, home, client sites, co-working spaces, or while traveling — and to all physical documents, removable media, and devices displaying XXAXX information.

## 3. Definitions

- **Clear Desk**: The practice of not leaving physical documents, removable media, or portable devices containing Confidential or Restricted information unattended and unsecured.
- **Clear Screen**: The practice of locking or logging out of a device when it is left unattended, and positioning screens to avoid unauthorized viewing.
- **Shoulder Surfing**: Observing a user's screen or keyboard, deliberately or incidentally, to view sensitive information or capture credentials.

## 4. Policy Statement

### 4.1 Clear Desk

Confidential and Restricted documents, removable media, and portable devices are not left unattended on desks, in meeting rooms, or in other accessible areas — they are secured in a locked drawer, cabinet, or equivalent when not in active use, and particularly at the end of the working day. This applies equally to home working spaces, particularly where the space is shared with others not authorized to view XXAXX information.

### 4.2 Clear Screen

Devices are locked (manually, or via automatic screen lock after a defined period of inactivity configured by IT) whenever left unattended, even briefly. Automatic screen lock timeouts are enforced through endpoint configuration wherever technically feasible, consistent with the Access Control Policy.

### 4.3 Printing and Physical Output

Printed material containing Confidential or Restricted information is collected from printers promptly and not left in shared output trays. Where practicable, secure/authenticated printing (requiring the user to release the print job at the printer) is used for Confidential/Restricted material.

### 4.4 Screen Positioning and Public Spaces

Screens are positioned, or privacy screens/filters used, to reduce the risk of shoulder surfing, particularly when working with Confidential/Restricted information in public spaces such as public transport, cafés, or airports, consistent with the Remote Working & BYOD Policy.

### 4.5 Whiteboards and Physical Displays

Whiteboards, flip charts, or other physical displays containing Confidential or Restricted information are erased or covered when not in use and, in particular, kept out of view during video calls and when visitors are present.

### 4.6 Removable Media

Removable media (e.g., USB drives) containing Confidential or Restricted information are stored securely when not in use, consistent with the Data Classification & Handling Policy, and are not left connected to unattended devices.

### 4.7 End of Day and Extended Absence

At the end of each working day, and before any extended absence from a workstation, users ensure their desk area is clear of Confidential/Restricted physical material and their device is locked or shut down as appropriate.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CISO | Owns this Policy; ensures automatic screen lock is enforced through technical configuration. |
| Facilities Manager | Supports lockable storage provision and secure/authenticated printing infrastructure. |
| Line Managers | Reinforce clear desk/clear screen practices within their teams; lead by example. |
| All Employees & Contractors | Comply with this Policy at all times and in all working locations. |

## 6. Compliance and Enforcement

Repeated or serious violations (e.g., Restricted documents left unattended in a public area) are addressed through the disciplinary process defined in the HR Security Policy. Compliance is monitored through periodic informal walkthroughs/spot checks in XXAXX offices and general observation.

## 7. Exceptions

There are no standing exceptions to this Policy; where a genuine operational constraint exists (e.g., a physical layout limitation), it should be raised with the CISO or Facilities Manager for a compensating solution.

## 8. Review and Update

This Policy is reviewed at least annually and upon significant change to XXAXX's office layout, remote working arrangements, or endpoint configuration standards.
