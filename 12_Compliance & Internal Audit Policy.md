## 1. Purpose

This Policy defines how XXAXX independently verifies that its information security controls are implemented as designed and are operating effectively, and how it monitors ongoing compliance with this policy suite, applicable law and regulation (including NIS2), and contractual obligations. Independent assurance closes the loop on the ISMS: policies and controls are only as good as the evidence that they actually work.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all information security policies, controls, and processes across XXAXX, including those operated by third parties on XXAXX's behalf where contractually auditable. It covers internal audit activity, compliance monitoring, management review, and coordination with external audits and regulatory assessments (e.g., NIS2 supervisory activity, ISO certification audits, customer security audits).

## 3. Definitions

- **Internal Audit**: An independent, objective assurance activity designed to evaluate and improve the effectiveness of controls, governance, and risk management.
- **Compliance Monitoring**: Ongoing, typically automated or lightweight, checking of adherence to defined policies, standards, and controls, distinct from formal audit.
- **Finding**: A documented instance of non-conformity, control weakness, or improvement opportunity identified through audit or monitoring.
- **Corrective Action**: A documented action taken to eliminate the cause of an identified non-conformity and prevent recurrence.
- **Statement of Applicability (SoA)**: The ISO/IEC 27001 document listing which Annex A controls apply to XXAXX and why, forming a key input to audit scope.
- **Management Review**: The formal, periodic review by the management body/CISO of the ISMS's continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness.

## 4. Policy Statement

### 4.1 Independence and Objectivity

Internal audit activity is performed with independence from the teams and functions being audited — auditors do not audit controls they themselves designed, implemented, or operate. Where XXAXX's size means full separation is impractical, independence is achieved through peer review, rotation of audit responsibility, or engagement of external audit resource for higher-risk areas.

### 4.2 Audit Program and Schedule

XXAXX maintains a risk-based internal audit program, reviewed and approved at least annually, that determines audit scope and frequency based on the criticality and risk rating of the area concerned (per the Risk Management Policy), time elapsed since the last audit, and any prior findings. At minimum, the audit program provides for periodic coverage of all topic-specific policies in this suite over a defined cycle (e.g., a rolling 2–3 year coverage plan for lower-risk areas, more frequent for higher-risk areas such as access control and incident management).

### 4.3 Audit Execution

Each audit follows a defined methodology: planning and scoping, evidence gathering (document review, control testing, interviews, technical verification), findings documentation, and reporting. Findings are rated by severity/risk, and each finding is assigned an owner and a target remediation date. Audit reports are provided to the CISO and relevant process owners, with significant findings reported to the management body.

### 4.4 Compliance Monitoring

In addition to periodic formal audit, XXAXX operates ongoing compliance monitoring using automated tooling where feasible (e.g., configuration compliance scanning, access review completion tracking, patch/vulnerability SLA compliance, policy attestation tracking) to provide continuous visibility between formal audit cycles.

### 4.5 Corrective Action Management

All audit findings and significant compliance monitoring exceptions are logged in a centrally tracked corrective action register, with defined owners and target dates. Overdue corrective actions are escalated to the CISO and, for high-severity or repeatedly overdue items, to the management body. Closure of a corrective action requires evidence that the underlying issue has been resolved, not merely that the target date has passed.

### 4.6 Statement of Applicability and Control Effectiveness Review

XXAXX maintains a Statement of Applicability documenting which controls (mapped across this policy suite, ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A, and NIS2 Article 21(2) measures) apply, their implementation status, and justification for any exclusions. Control effectiveness is assessed periodically — not merely whether a control exists, but whether it is achieving its intended risk-reduction outcome — feeding into the Risk Management Policy's residual risk assessment.

### 4.7 Management Review

The CISO conducts a formal Management Review of the ISMS at least annually, covering: status of previous actions, changes in internal/external context, risk assessment and treatment status, audit results, incident trends, non-conformities and corrective actions, performance against security objectives, and resource adequacy. Management Review outputs, including any decisions on changes to the ISMS, are documented and reported to the management body.

### 4.8 External Audits and Regulatory Assessments

XXAXX coordinates support for external audits (e.g., ISO 27001 certification audits, customer/supplier security audits) and regulatory supervisory activity (e.g., NIS2 competent authority inspections or information requests) through the CISO, ensuring timely, accurate provision of requested evidence and appropriate legal review of formal regulatory correspondence.

### 4.9 Evidence Retention

Audit evidence, reports, corrective action records, and Management Review outputs are retained for a minimum of [RETENTION PERIOD] to support both internal governance needs and any regulatory inquiry into XXAXX's historical compliance posture.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Management Body | Approves the audit program; receives significant findings and Management Review outputs; holds the CISO accountable for remediation of systemic issues. |
| CISO | Owns this Policy; approves the risk-based audit plan; conducts Management Review; oversees the corrective action register. |
| Internal Audit Function (or delegated resource) | Executes the audit program independently; documents and reports findings. |
| Process / Control Owners | Provide evidence and access for audit; remediate findings within agreed timelines; participate in Management Review inputs relevant to their area. |
| Compliance / Risk Function (if separate from CISO) | Operates ongoing compliance monitoring tooling; maintains the Statement of Applicability. |
| All Employees | Cooperate with audit activity; provide accurate information when requested. |

## 6. Compliance and Enforcement

Failure to remediate an audit finding within its agreed timeline without an approved extension, or obstruction of audit activity (e.g., withholding evidence, restricting access), is treated as a policy breach and escalated to the CISO and, where persistent, the management body. Adherence to this Policy is itself subject to periodic independent review (e.g., via external ISO certification audit or peer review of the internal audit function).

## 7. Exceptions

Extensions to corrective action deadlines must be approved by the CISO, documented with rationale and a revised date, and tracked; repeated extensions on the same finding are escalated to the management body.

## 8. Review and Update

This Policy and the internal audit program methodology are reviewed at least annually and upon significant change to XXAXX's risk profile, organizational structure, or applicable regulatory requirements.
