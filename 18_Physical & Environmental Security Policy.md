## 1. Purpose

This Policy defines how XXAXX protects its facilities, equipment, and the information they house from physical and environmental threats — unauthorized physical access, theft, damage, fire, power failure, and other environmental hazards. Physical security is a foundational control: even the strongest technical controls can be undermined if an attacker (or an accident) can gain unauthorized physical access to XXAXX's premises or equipment.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all XXAXX-occupied or -controlled facilities, including offices, and any on-premises server/network rooms. Where XXAXX relies on third-party data center or colocation facilities, this Policy governs XXAXX's due diligence and contractual expectations of those providers' physical security, coordinated with the Supplier & Third-Party Security Policy; the direct operational controls in this Policy apply to XXAXX-controlled space. It applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors.

## 3. Definitions

- **Secure Area**: A physical space with defined entry controls, housing sensitive information assets, systems, or activities (e.g., a server room, comms room).
- **Physical Access Control**: Mechanisms (e.g., badge readers, locks, security personnel) restricting physical entry to a facility or area.
- **Environmental Control**: Systems (e.g., fire suppression, HVAC, power backup) that protect facilities and equipment from environmental hazards.
- **Visitor**: Any individual on XXAXX premises who is not an employee or authorized contractor with standing access.
- **Tailgating**: Unauthorized entry to a secured area by following an authorized individual through an access-controlled door without independent authentication.

## 4. Policy Statement

### 4.1 Physical Security Perimeter

XXAXX defines and maintains a physical security perimeter around its facilities, with entry points controlled through appropriate mechanisms (e.g., badge access, reception/staffed entry, locks) proportionate to the sensitivity of the area and the assets it contains.

### 4.2 Access Control to Facilities and Secure Areas

Access to XXAXX facilities is granted only to authorized individuals, using individually assigned access credentials (badges/key fobs) wherever practicable rather than shared keys, consistent with the identity principles in the Access Control Policy. Secure Areas (e.g., server/comms rooms) are subject to additional restricted access, limited to individuals whose role requires entry, with access logged and reviewed periodically alongside logical access reviews.

### 4.3 Visitor Management

Visitors are required to sign in, be issued visible visitor identification, and are escorted or otherwise supervised while in non-public areas, particularly Secure Areas. Visitor access is logged, including time of entry and exit and the individual or team hosting the visit.

### 4.4 Tailgating Prevention

Employees are trained (per the Security Awareness & Training Policy) not to allow tailgating into access-controlled areas, and to challenge or report unfamiliar individuals in restricted spaces. Where risk warrants, physical controls such as mantraps, turnstiles, or security personnel are used to reduce reliance on individual vigilance alone.

### 4.5 Equipment Siting and Protection

Equipment housing or processing Confidential or Restricted information is sited to reduce risks from environmental threats and unauthorized access — for example, not positioned where it is visible or accessible from public areas, and located away from obvious environmental risks (e.g., water sources, ground-floor flood risk) where feasible.

### 4.6 Environmental Controls

Secure Areas housing critical IT equipment are protected by environmental controls proportionate to the criticality of the equipment they house, which may include: fire detection and suppression systems, temperature and humidity monitoring/control, and water leak detection. Environmental controls are tested and maintained per a defined schedule.

### 4.7 Power

Critical IT equipment is protected against power fluctuation and outage through uninterruptible power supply (UPS) and, where warranted by criticality, backup generator capacity, coordinated with the Business Continuity Policy and Backup & Disaster Recovery Policy's RTO requirements. Power backup capacity is tested periodically.

### 4.8 Cabling Security

Power and data cabling supporting critical systems is protected from interception, interference, or damage, routed to avoid unauthorized physical access where feasible, and, where practicable, separated from general power cabling to reduce interference risk.

### 4.9 Equipment Maintenance

Equipment is maintained per manufacturer/vendor specifications to ensure continued availability and integrity. Maintenance performed by third parties is supervised or verified, and any equipment leaving XXAXX premises for maintenance/repair that may contain Confidential or Restricted data is handled per the Asset Management Policy's secure disposal/data-sanitization requirements before release, where feasible, or covered by an appropriate confidentiality agreement with the maintenance provider.

### 4.10 Secure Disposal of Physical Media and Equipment

Physical media and equipment containing Confidential or Restricted data are securely destroyed or sanitized before disposal or reuse, consistent with the Asset Management Policy and Data Classification & Handling Policy, with disposal evidence retained for Restricted data.

### 4.11 Working in Secure Areas

Personnel and third parties working within Secure Areas follow defined health, safety, and confidentiality expectations, and photography or recording within Secure Areas is prohibited without specific authorization.

### 4.12 Delivery and Loading Areas

Delivery and loading areas are, where feasible, isolated from areas housing information processing facilities to prevent unauthorized access via goods-in points, and incoming deliveries are inspected for potential hazards before being brought into secure areas.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Facilities Manager | Owns day-to-day physical security and environmental control operations; manages visitor management and access badge administration. |
| CISO | Owns this Policy; ensures physical security controls for areas housing Confidential/Restricted information meet risk-appropriate standards. |
| IT Operations | Maintains equipment sited within Secure Areas; coordinates environmental control requirements for IT equipment with Facilities. |
| Reception / Security Personnel | Manage visitor sign-in, identification, and escort; monitor for suspicious activity. |
| All Employees & Contractors | Do not permit tailgating; challenge or report unfamiliar individuals in restricted areas; follow Secure Area rules. |
| Third-Party Data Center / Colocation Providers | Provide and evidence physical security controls per contractual requirements, per the Supplier & Third-Party Security Policy. |

## 6. Compliance and Enforcement

Unauthorized access to a Secure Area, failure to challenge or report suspected tailgating, or removal of equipment/media without authorization is treated as a policy breach and handled per the HR Security Policy's disciplinary process where applicable. Compliance is monitored through access log review, visitor log audits, and periodic physical security walkthroughs.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions to standard physical access requirements (e.g., temporary access for a specific project) must be approved by the Facilities Manager or CISO, time-bound, and logged.

## 8. Review and Update

This Policy is reviewed at least annually and upon significant change to XXAXX's facilities, environmental risk profile, or applicable regulatory requirements.
