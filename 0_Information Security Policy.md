## 1. Purpose

This Information Security Policy establishes XXAXX's overarching commitment to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of its information assets, network and information systems, and the services it provides. It sets the governance framework under which all topic-specific security policies operate.

This Policy ensures XXAXX implements appropriate and proportionate technical, operational, and organizational measures to manage the risks posed to the security of its network and information systems, and to prevent or minimize the impact of incidents on recipients of its services and on other services.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- All employees, contractors, temporary staff, interns, and third parties acting on behalf of XXAXX;
- All information assets owned, leased, or processed by XXAXX, including data, hardware, software, cloud services, and network infrastructure;
- All locations from which XXAXX's information systems are accessed, including offices, remote working locations, and cloud environments;
- All entities in XXAXX's supply chain to the extent defined in the Supplier & Third-Party Security Policy.

This Policy applies organization-wide and takes precedence over any conflicting departmental practice. This Policy and its supporting policies constitute the primary evidence of XXAXX's information security governance framework.

## 3. Definitions

- **ISMS**: Information Security Management System — the overall framework of policies, processes, and controls under which information security is managed.
- **Major Incident**: An incident that has caused, or is capable of causing, severe operational disruption or financial loss, or that affects other persons by causing considerable material or non-material damage.
- **Management Body**: The board of directors, executive committee, or equivalent governing body with ultimate accountability for XXAXX's cybersecurity risk management.
- **Topic-Specific Policy**: Any of the supporting policies listed in Section 9 that elaborate on a specific security domain within the scope of this Policy.

## 4. Policy Statement

### 4.1 Governance & Leadership Commitment

XXAXX's management body approves this Policy, allocates adequate resources to information security, and is accountable for the organization's cybersecurity risk management. Members of the management body undertake training to gain sufficient knowledge and skills to identify and assess cybersecurity risks and evaluate risk-management practices, and encourage similar training for employees on a regular basis.

### 4.2 Risk-Based Approach

XXAXX adopts an all-hazards, risk-based approach to information security, proportionate to its size, exposure to risk, and the likelihood and severity of incidents, including their societal and economic impact. Risks are identified, assessed, treated, and monitored in accordance with the Risk Management Policy.

### 4.3 Policy Framework

This Policy is supported by a suite of topic-specific policies (see Section 9), structured according to the four ISO/IEC 27002:2022 control themes: Organizational, People, Physical, and Technological. Each topic-specific policy:

- Has a named owner accountable for its maintenance;
- Is reviewed at least annually or upon significant change;
- Is mapped to relevant regulatory articles, ISO/IEC 27001 clauses, ISO/IEC 27002 controls, and NIST CSF v2.0 categories;
- Is approved through the same governance process as this Policy.

### 4.4 Objectives

Through this Policy and its supporting framework, XXAXX aims to:

- Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information and network/information systems;
- Ensure business continuity and minimize the impact of incidents on service recipients;
- Meet all applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations;
- Foster a culture of security awareness and shared responsibility across the organization;
- Continuously improve the ISMS through monitoring, audit, and lessons learned.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Management Body (Board / Executive Committee) | Approves this policy and the ISMS framework; oversees cybersecurity risk management; approves risk acceptance at strategic level; undertakes training; is accountable for organizational compliance. |
| Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | Owns this policy and the overall ISMS; coordinates the topic-specific policy suite; reports risk posture and incidents to management; leads the compliance program. |
| Information Security / IT Team | Implements technical and organizational controls; operates security tooling; supports policy owners of topic-specific policies; maintains the risk register. |
| Policy Owners (per topic-specific policy) | Maintain, review, and update their respective policy; monitor compliance within their domain; report exceptions to the CISO. |
| Line Managers | Ensure staff awareness and compliance within their teams; escalate incidents and policy exceptions. |
| All Employees, Contractors & Third Parties | Comply with this policy and all supporting policies; complete mandatory awareness training; report suspected incidents without delay. |
| Internal Audit / Compliance Function | Independently assesses the effectiveness of security measures; reports findings to management body. |

## 6. Compliance and Enforcement

Compliance with this Policy and all supporting topic-specific policies is mandatory. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract, and, where applicable, referral to civil or criminal authorities. Compliance is monitored through internal audit, technical control monitoring, and management review, in accordance with the Compliance & Internal Audit Policy.

XXAXX recognizes that members of the management body can be held personally accountable for governance failures in this domain, and that competent authorities may impose administrative fines, temporary bans on management functions, and other supervisory measures for non-compliance with applicable cybersecurity legislation.

## 7. Exceptions

Any request for exception to this Policy or a supporting topic-specific policy must be submitted in writing to the CISO, include a business justification and compensating controls, and be time-bound and formally approved before taking effect. All approved exceptions are logged in the risk register and reviewed at each policy review cycle.

## 8. Review and Update

This Policy is reviewed at least annually by the CISO and approved by the management body. It is also reviewed following major incidents, material changes to the threat landscape, organizational structure, applicable legislation, or findings from internal/external audits.
