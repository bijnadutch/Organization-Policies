## Purpose

This index is the single entry point into XXAXX's Information Security Policy Suite. It lists every policy and supporting document in the suite, its owner, its domain, and the primary NIS2 Article 21(2) measure(s) it implements, so that any reader — an employee, an auditor, or a regulator — can quickly locate the right document. Each individual policy carries its own full mapping to NIS2, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 27002:2022, and NIST CSF v2.0 in its own Appendix A; this index summarizes that structure at the suite level rather than repeating every clause.

The suite is built around one governance umbrella (Policy #0) and 26 topic-specific policies organized into the four ISO/IEC 27002:2022 control themes — **Organizational, People, Physical, and Technological** — with two supporting documents (a methodology and a technical procedure) attached to their parent policies.

---

## How to Use This Index

- **New to the suite?** Start with **Policy #0 — Information Security Policy**, which explains the governance model and lists every supporting policy.
- **Looking for a specific topic?** Use the domain tables below, or the topic-to-policy quick lookup in Section 4.
- **Responding to an incident?** Go directly to **Policy #8a — Security Incident Response Procedure** and **Policy #9 — NIS2 Incident Reporting Procedure**.
- **Preparing for audit?** **Policy #12 — Compliance & Internal Audit Policy** governs the audit program itself; each policy's Appendix A provides the control mapping an auditor will want to trace.

## 7. Outstanding Items Before Formal Adoption

The following items were deliberately left open across the suite and should be resolved before this framework is formally approved and put into effect:

- **`[DATE]` placeholders**: Effective dates, review dates, and revision history dates throughout every document.
- **Named approvers**: Role titles (CISO, Head of HR, etc.) are used throughout; substitute actual names/titles if XXAXX prefers named accountability in the Document Control tables.
- **Financial impact thresholds** in the Risk Assessment Methodology (Policy #1a) — `[X]/[Y]/[Z]/[W]` — need sizing to XXAXX's actual revenue/scale.
- **Competent authority / national CSIRT identity** in the NIS2 Incident Reporting Procedure (Policy #9) — depends on XXAXX's member state(s) of operation and sector classification (essential vs. important entity), which was left unconfirmed earlier in scoping.
- **Retention periods** marked `[RETENTION PERIOD]` in Policy #9 and Policy #24 — need to be set against XXAXX's legal/regulatory requirements.
- **Supporting operational documents** referenced throughout but not yet drafted (e.g., Cryptographic Standards, Endpoint Protection Standard, Cloud Security Baseline, Standard Change Catalog, Business Impact Analysis, Business Continuity Plans, Disaster Recovery Plans per system) — these are working documents that sit beneath the policy layer and were intentionally scoped out of this policy-level engagement.
