## 1. Purpose

This Policy defines the information security requirements applicable throughout the employment (and equivalent contractor/temporary worker) lifecycle at XXAXX — from pre-employment screening through onboarding, ongoing employment, role change, and termination. People are consistently one of the most significant factors in an organization's security posture, whether through error, social engineering, or, rarely, deliberate misuse of access; this Policy ensures security expectations are clear, communicated, and enforced at every stage.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all XXAXX employees, temporary staff, interns, and — to the extent specified in individual contracts — contractors and consultants engaged to work on XXAXX's behalf. It covers the full employment lifecycle: pre-employment screening, onboarding, ongoing employment (including role changes), and termination/offboarding.

## 3. Definitions

- **Screening / Background Check**: A pre-employment verification process assessing a candidate's identity, employment history, qualifications, and, where legally permitted and role-appropriate, criminal record or financial background.
- **Confidentiality / Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**: A contractual commitment to protect XXAXX's confidential information during and after employment.
- **Disciplinary Process**: XXAXX's formal process for addressing policy violations or misconduct by employees.
- **Joiner / Mover / Leaver (JML)**: The three key employment lifecycle transitions that trigger access and asset management actions.

## 4. Policy Statement

### 4.1 Pre-Employment Screening

XXAXX performs background screening for prospective employees and, where contractually specified, contractors, proportionate to the sensitivity and access level of the role. Screening depth (identity verification, employment/reference checks, right-to-work verification, and, for roles with access to Restricted data, privileged systems, or financial functions, more extensive checks such as criminal record checks where legally permitted) is defined per role category and conducted in compliance with applicable employment and data protection law. Screening is completed, or a documented risk-based exception is approved, before access to Confidential or Restricted systems/data is granted.

### 4.2 Terms and Conditions of Employment

Employment contracts and equivalent contractor agreements include information security responsibilities, confidentiality obligations (including post-termination survival where applicable), acceptable use expectations, and consequences of non-compliance. Role-specific security responsibilities are reflected in job descriptions for roles with elevated access or security responsibilities (e.g., IT administrators, developers, finance).

### 4.3 Onboarding

New employees and contractors complete security onboarding before or promptly upon commencing work, covering: this Policy suite (or a role-appropriate summary), the Acceptable Use Policy, and mandatory security awareness training per the Security Awareness & Training Policy. Account and asset provisioning follows the Access Control Policy and Asset Management Policy, granted only to the extent required for the individual's role, and only once required agreements (confidentiality, acceptable use acknowledgment) are signed.

### 4.4 Ongoing Employment and Role Changes

Security responsibilities and access are reviewed whenever an individual changes role, consistent with the Access Control Policy's requirement to remove access associated with a previous role rather than accumulate it. Line managers are responsible for identifying and communicating role changes to HR and IT promptly.

### 4.5 Security Awareness and Acknowledgment

All personnel formally acknowledge, at onboarding and periodically thereafter (at minimum annually), that they have read, understood, and will comply with this Policy suite. Ongoing security awareness training is addressed in the Security Awareness & Training Policy.

### 4.6 Disciplinary Process

Violations of this Policy suite are addressed through XXAXX's disciplinary process, applied consistently and proportionate to the severity, intent, and impact of the violation, in coordination with HR and, where relevant, Legal. The disciplinary process considers whether a violation was inadvertent (which may warrant coaching/retraining) versus deliberate or negligent (which may warrant formal disciplinary action up to termination), and is applied without regard to the individual's seniority.

### 4.7 Remote and Contract Workers

Security expectations under this Policy apply equally to remote employees and contract/temporary workers, with additional requirements for remote working arrangements addressed in the Remote Working & BYOD Policy. Contracts with staffing agencies or contractor firms specify the security screening, confidentiality, and acceptable use obligations that apply to individuals they place with XXAXX.

### 4.8 Termination and Offboarding

HR notifies IT and the relevant line manager of a termination or resignation as early as possible to allow planned, orderly offboarding, coordinated with the Access Control Policy (access revocation) and Asset Management Policy (asset return). For involuntary terminations or departures assessed as higher risk, access is revoked at or before the point the individual is notified, rather than at end of the working day. Exit interviews reinforce ongoing confidentiality obligations that survive termination.

### 4.9 Post-Employment Obligations

Confidentiality and, where applicable, non-compete or non-solicitation obligations that survive termination are clearly communicated to departing personnel and remain enforceable per the individual's employment contract and applicable law.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Head of HR | Owns the employment lifecycle process; ensures screening, contractual, and offboarding requirements are met; notifies IT/CISO promptly of joiners, movers, and leavers. |
| CISO | Owns the security content of this Policy; defines role-based screening and access requirements; oversees security awareness acknowledgment tracking. |
| Line Managers | Identify and communicate role changes and departures promptly; reinforce security expectations within their teams. |
| IT / Information Security Team | Executes access provisioning/de-provisioning and asset management actions triggered by HR notifications. |
| Legal | Advises on employment law compliance for screening, disciplinary action, and post-termination obligations. |
| All Employees & Contractors | Comply with this Policy and acknowledge understanding at onboarding and periodically thereafter. |

## 6. Compliance and Enforcement

Failure to complete required screening prior to granting Confidential/Restricted access, or failure by a line manager to promptly report a role change or departure, is treated as a policy breach. Compliance is monitored through screening completion tracking, onboarding/offboarding checklists, and internal audit.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions to standard screening or onboarding sequencing (e.g., urgent hire pending completion of a background check) must be approved by the CISO and Head of HR jointly, documented with compensating controls (e.g., restricted interim access) and a resolution timeline.

## 8. Review and Update

This Policy is reviewed at least annually and upon significant change to employment law, organizational structure, or HR/IT processes.
