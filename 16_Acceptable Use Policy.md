## 1. Purpose

This Policy sets out the rules governing everyday use of XXAXX's information systems, devices, network, email, internet access, and other IT resources by its workforce. It translates the higher-level principles in the Information Security Policy and Asset Management Policy into plain, practical rules that every user can understand and follow, reducing risk arising from careless, inappropriate, or unauthorized use.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all XXAXX employees, contractors, and temporary staff who use XXAXX-provided IT assets, accounts, or network access, and to personally owned devices authorized to access XXAXX systems or data under the Remote Working & BYOD Policy. It covers use of devices, accounts, email, internet, messaging/collaboration tools, and cloud services provided or authorized by XXAXX.

## 3. Definitions

- **XXAXX Assets**: IT devices, accounts, software, network access, and cloud services provided or authorized by XXAXX for business use.
- **Incidental Personal Use**: Limited personal use of XXAXX assets that does not interfere with work duties, incur meaningful cost, or create security or legal risk.
- **Prohibited Use**: Use of XXAXX assets in a manner explicitly disallowed under this Policy.

## 4. Policy Statement

### 4.1 General Principle

XXAXX assets are provided primarily for business purposes in support of XXAXX's objectives. Reasonable incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, consume significant resources, violate this Policy or applicable law, or create security, legal, or reputational risk for XXAXX. Users have no expectation of privacy in their use of XXAXX assets, subject to applicable employment and data protection law and any separate monitoring notice provided to employees.

### 4.2 Acceptable Use

Users are expected to:

- Use XXAXX assets primarily for authorized business purposes;
- Protect authentication credentials and never share accounts or passwords;
- Lock devices when unattended and follow the Clear Desk & Clear Screen Policy;
- Keep software and devices updated as directed by IT, and not disable or circumvent security controls (e.g., endpoint protection, patching agents);
- Handle information in accordance with its classification, per the Data Classification & Handling Policy;
- Report lost or stolen devices, suspected phishing, malware, or other security concerns immediately;
- Use only approved software, cloud services, and storage locations for business data.

### 4.3 Prohibited Use

Users must not:

- Use XXAXX assets to engage in illegal activity, harassment, discrimination, or the creation/distribution of offensive material;
- Install unauthorized or unlicensed software, or connect unauthorized hardware/removable media to XXAXX systems, except where permitted under the Asset Management Policy's exception process;
- Attempt to bypass, disable, or circumvent security controls, monitoring, or access restrictions;
- Access, alter, or disclose information they are not authorized to access, including attempting to access another user's account or data without authorization;
- Use XXAXX assets or accounts for personal commercial activity, political campaigning, or activity that creates a conflict of interest;
- Transmit Confidential or Restricted information through unapproved channels (e.g., personal email, unsanctioned file-sharing or messaging services), per the Data Classification & Handling Policy;
- Use unauthorized cloud storage, AI, or SaaS tools to process XXAXX or customer data without prior approval through the Supplier & Third-Party Security Policy process;
- Engage in excessive personal use that interferes with work duties or consumes significant XXAXX resources (e.g., bandwidth-intensive streaming or file-sharing unrelated to work).

### 4.4 Email and Messaging

Business email and messaging/collaboration tools are used primarily for business communication. Users exercise caution with unsolicited attachments and links, verify unexpected or unusual requests (particularly those involving payments, credentials, or urgent action) through a secondary channel, and report suspected phishing per the Security Awareness & Training Policy. Auto-forwarding of business email to personal accounts is prohibited.

### 4.5 Internet Access

Internet access provided by XXAXX is intended primarily for business purposes. Access to categories of content that are illegal, offensive, or that pose a known security risk (e.g., known malicious sites) may be filtered or blocked by XXAXX's security controls. Users should not attempt to circumvent web filtering or proxy controls.

### 4.6 Use of Cloud Services and AI Tools

Users may only use cloud services and AI/productivity tools that have been approved by IT/Information Security to process XXAXX business data, consistent with the Cloud Security Policy and Supplier & Third-Party Security Policy. Confidential or Restricted data must not be entered into unapproved AI tools or public-facing services, given the risk of that data being retained, used for third-party model training, or otherwise exposed outside XXAXX's control.

### 4.7 Monitoring

XXAXX may monitor use of its assets (e.g., email, internet access, device activity) for security, legal, and operational purposes, to the extent permitted by applicable law and in accordance with any separate employee monitoring notice or works council/employee representative consultation where legally required. Monitoring is conducted proportionately and primarily for security and compliance purposes rather than routine surveillance of individuals.

### 4.8 Social Media

Personal use of social media on XXAXX assets or during work time should be reasonable and not interfere with duties. Users must not disclose Confidential or Restricted XXAXX information on social media, and should be mindful that public statements can be attributed to XXAXX even when posted from a personal account, particularly when discussing work-related matters.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CISO | Owns this Policy; defines approved tools/services lists; oversees monitoring controls proportionate to risk. |
| IT / Information Security Team | Implements technical controls supporting this Policy (filtering, endpoint protection, approved software lists); investigates suspected violations. |
| Line Managers | Reinforce acceptable use expectations; escalate suspected violations to HR/CISO. |
| HR | Handles disciplinary process for confirmed violations, per the HR Security Policy. |
| All Users | Comply with this Policy; report suspected violations or security concerns. |

## 6. Compliance and Enforcement

Violations of this Policy are addressed through the disciplinary process defined in the HR Security Policy, proportionate to the severity, intent, and impact of the violation. Certain violations (e.g., unauthorized access to data, use of XXAXX assets for illegal activity) may also result in referral to law enforcement. Compliance is monitored through security tooling (e.g., DLP, web filtering logs where deployed) and periodic review.

## 7. Exceptions

Requests for exceptions (e.g., a specific business need for a non-standard tool) must be submitted to IT/Information Security, assessed for security risk consistent with the Supplier & Third-Party Security Policy where a new service is involved, and approved before use.

## 8. Review and Update

This Policy is reviewed at least annually and upon significant change to XXAXX's technology environment, approved tool list, or applicable law.
